# Known Circuits in Mechanistic Interpretability

A curated table of well-established circuits discovered in transformer language models, covering tasks, models, components, and source papers.

---

| # | Circuit Name | Task / Behavior | Model(s) | Key Components | Paper / Source | Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Induction Circuit** | In-context learning: completing repeating patterns [A][B]…[A]→[B] | Small attention-only Transformers, GPT-2, most LLMs | Previous Token Head (layer L), Induction Head (layer L+1); two attention heads working in sequence | Elhage et al., *A Mathematical Framework for Transformer Circuits*, Transformer Circuits Thread, 2021; Olsson et al., *In-context Learning and Induction Heads*, 2022 | https://transformer-circuits.pub/2022/in-context-learning-and-induction-heads/index.html |
| 2 | **Indirect Object Identification (IOI) Circuit** | Identifying the indirect object in sentences like "When Mary and John went to the store, John gave a drink to ___" | GPT-2 Small | 26–28 attention heads in 7 classes: Name Mover Heads, Duplicate Token Heads, S-Inhibition Heads, Induction Heads, Backup Name Mover Heads, Previous Token Heads, Negative Name Mover Heads | Wang et al., *Interpretability in the Wild: A Circuit for Indirect Object Identification in GPT-2 Small*, ICLR 2023 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.00593 |

## Notes

- **Model abbreviations**: GPT-2 Small = 12 layers, 117M params; Chinchilla 70B = DeepMind's 70B model; Pythia = EleutherAI's open-weight suite.
- **Component types**: Attention heads are denoted layer.head (e.g., 10.7 = layer 10, head 7). MLP layers are feed-forward sublayers.
- **Circuit granularity**: Some circuits (rows 1–15) are described at the attention-head/MLP level; row 19 operates at the SAE-feature level, offering finer granularity.
- The IOI circuit (row 2) has been the most widely used benchmark for validating new circuit discovery methods, including ACDC, EAP, IPE, and PAP.
